## Summary
ADR-018 is a faithful record of the human's 2026-11-24 decisions (the direct-decision and I01-transition
log entries) — content matches well. The problems are impact and consistency: ADR-018 has no Impact/Options
section the flow expects, `architecture.md` was never updated to know design reviews exist, and REQ-091
asks `spine-guard` to enforce a "topic-mode" read scope that the guard's `agent_type`-only model (ADR-010)
cannot express. No external-fact claims in this ADR needed web verification.
